Ball Fight bookmarklet

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

🥊 Ball Fight

Click it on any webpage. A betting panel appears top right and the balls fight on top of the page. Click the bookmark again to close.

Betting (bettcoins)

  • You start with 200 bettcoins. Pick a fighter, pick an amount, hit FIGHT!
  • If your pick wins you get your bet times the number of fighters (e.g. 3 fighters = 3x back).
  • Spend bettcoins in the Shop to add new fighters with new powers. Each round uses up to 5 of your fighters.
  • Your coins and fighters are saved in this browser, per website (bookmarklets can't share storage between sites).

Powers (auto-used by default, or press the keys)

  • G / H Bird: wing dash / statue (turns nearest enemy to stone)
  • F / N Pat: laser eyes / NUKE
  • J / K Smiley: strafing run / bombing run (plane flyover)
  • X Ninja: shuriken storm (150)
  • O Ghost: phase, invulnerable 3s (200)
  • B Dragon: fire breath (300)
  • L Wizard: lightning on every enemy (300)
  • R Robot: repair 35 HP (350)
  • C / V / Z Hunna (200,000): mega blast on every enemy / 5s shield / smash. 500 HP, very overpowered

Admin panel

Click the Admin button in the panel and enter the password to add bettcoins, give fighters, change game speed, or reset progress.
